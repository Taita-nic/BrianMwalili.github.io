About me

I’m Brian, a Kenyan engineering student finishing my BSc in 2027. My training covers solar, wind, hydro, bio-energy, energy storage and energy policy, and I’ve worked hands-on in power distribution and substations at Kenya Power.

I like building things that cut wasted energy, such as a solar lighting system that only switches on when it’s needed. I’m AutoCAD and Revit certified, and I also make augmented reality art.

I speak English and Swahili fluently, and I’m based in Ruiru.

Projects

Smart solar lighting

A small solar lighting prototype: a 3.5 W panel, 12 V 7 Ah battery and PWM charge controller power an Arduino-controlled LED load. Two light sensors and a motion sensor switch the light on only when a room is dark and occupied, with live readings on an LCD. I calibrated it across daylight, overcast, evening and night, and tested it for a week.

  • Solar PV
